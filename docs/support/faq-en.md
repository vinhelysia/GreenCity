# GreenCity support knowledge base

This is the approved answer source for GreenCity support. The assistant may
state **CURRENT** items as available, must label **DEMO** items as prototypes,
and must not promise dates for **ROADMAP** items.

## Accounts

### How do I register or sign in? — CURRENT

Choose **Register** to create an account with an email address and password, or
**Sign in** if you already have one. Never send a password, session token, or
payment information through support chat.

### I cannot access my account — HUMAN HANDOFF

The assistant must not reset passwords or ask for a password. Transfer the
conversation to a support agent for verification and guidance.

## Selling recyclables

### How do I sell recyclables? — CURRENT

Sign in, open **Sell recyclables**, choose a material, enter the estimated
weight and pickup address, and upload one current photo. GreenCity provides a
quote within the published price range; the seller can review and accept it on
the same page.

### When do I earn points from a sale? — CURRENT

Points are awarded only after an administrator confirms that the transaction is
complete. The current rule is one point per VND 1,000 received by the seller,
with at least one point for a completed transaction.

## Marketplace and Buyer Pass

### How do I reserve a listing? — CURRENT

Sign in, open **Marketplace**, and select **Reserve listing**. The account needs
an active Buyer Pass. Only one buyer can successfully reserve each listing.

### How much is the Buyer Pass? — CURRENT WITH CONDITIONS

The Buyer Pass is a one-time VND 50,000 payment for 30 days and does not renew
automatically. payOS checkout is available only when the environment is fully
configured. GreenCity must not claim a real payment works until a checkout and
signed webhook have been observed with a real merchant account.

### I paid but my pass is not active — HUMAN HANDOFF

Do not ask for card details, passwords, OTPs, or an unredacted receipt. Transfer
the conversation to a support agent. The assistant must not promise a refund or
manual activation.

## Dumping-site reports

### How do I report a dumping site? — CURRENT

Sign in, open **Community contribution**, select a map location or use the
device location, add a description and current photo, then submit. An
administrator must verify the report.

### How many points does a verified report earn? — CURRENT

An administrator-verified report earns 50 points. Pending or rejected reports
earn no points, and one report can be rewarded only once.

## Rewards

### Where can I see my balance and points history? — CURRENT

Sign in and open **Account & Rewards**. The balance is calculated from the
points ledger and each awarded event appears in its history. The assistant must
not guess a user's balance from chat content.

### Can GreenCity points be withdrawn as cash? — CURRENT

No. GreenCity points are internal rewards, not money. They cannot be traded,
transferred, or withdrawn as cash.

### What can I buy with points? — DEMO

The catalog currently displays prototype coupons for Starbucks, Highlands
Coffee, Jollibee, KFC, EVN electricity-bill support, and municipal-water bill
support. The catalog is database-backed, but the coupons remain contest demos.

### Can I use a demo coupon? — DEMO

No. `DEMO-ONLY` codes are not valid, previewing one does not deduct points, and
GreenCity does not claim a current affiliation with the named organizations.

### When will real redemption launch? — ROADMAP

Real redemption requires signed partner agreements and provider integration.
There is no confirmed launch date.

## Human handoff

Transfer immediately for account access, payments, refunds, transaction
disputes, suspected fraud, personal-data requests, or any question not answered
confidently by this document. Do not promise a response time unless GreenCity
has published an SLA.
